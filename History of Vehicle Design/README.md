## Version 2
These following versions of the robot have a complete set of sensors. With these, these robots were programmed to finish both Open and Obstacle Challenge. The [Version 2.3](https://github.com/AbeBuck/BSU-Spartan-Team_FE-2024/blob/main/History%20of%20Vehicle%20Design/Vehicle%20v2.3.jpg) of the robot was used to compete in the National Competition. The team dominated the tournament and was declared as the National Champion. The said version of the robot is able to perform three laps and park in Obstacle Challenge, which it successfully did during the contest, though it can only park partly in the parking area.

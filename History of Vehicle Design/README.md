## Version 2
The following versions of the robot are equipped with a complete set of sensors. With these capabilities, the robots were programmed to successfully complete both the Open Challenge and the Obstacle Challenge. In [Version 2.1](https://github.com/AbeBuck/BSU-Spartan-Team_FE-2024/blob/main/History%20of%20Vehicle%20Design/Vehicle%20v2.1.jpg), we attempted to integrate the Huskylens camera. However, due to limitations in our current programming platform, we transitioned to using the AISteam Camera instead. The [Version 2.3](https://github.com/AbeBuck/BSU-Spartan-Team_FE-2024/blob/main/History%20of%20Vehicle%20Design/Vehicle%20v2.3.jpg) of the robot was utilized in the National Competition, where the team excelled and was crowned National Champion. This version demonstrated its ability to complete three laps and execute parking in the Obstacle Challenge. While it successfully parked during the contest, it could only partially fit into the designated parking area.

## Version 1
The first versions of the robot focused primarily on conceptualizing the design to be used in the National Competition. We planned to integrate a rotating mechanism for the distance sensor and camera, allowing a 180-degree rotation. However, the robot did not have a camera at the time due to the unavailability of one at the school. Without a camera, these early versions were only programmed to complete the Open Challenge. In [Version 1.2](https://github.com/AbeBuck/BSU-Spartan-Team_FE-2024/blob/main/History%20of%20Vehicle%20Design/Vehicle%20v1.2.jpg), we began experimenting with adding gears intended for use in the Obstacle Challenge, which would be implemented once a camera became available.
